Created `/app/solution.sparql`.

The query:
- Selects full professors whose role begins with `Professor`.
- Requires at least one department in an EU member state as of 2025-08-16.
- Counts distinct currently enrolled students per department, excluding students who graduated on or before the reference date.
- Returns all countries where each qualifying professor works using the requested `GROUP_CONCAT`.

Expected qualifying professors from the supplied data:
- Orfeas Menis
- Pepe Attanasio
- Aristotle Tympas
