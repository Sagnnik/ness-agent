Created `/app/solution.sparql`.

The query:
- Selects full professors whose role starts with `Professor`.
- Requires at least one workplace in an EU member state.
- Counts distinct currently enrolled students per worked-in department.
- Uses `2025-08-16` for enrollment and graduation-date checks.
- Returns all workplace countries via the requested `GROUP_CONCAT`.

Independent data validation identifies these qualifying professors:
- Aristotle Tympas — GR
- Orfeas Menis — GR, US
- Pepe Attanasio — GR, PT

Direct SPARQL execution was unavailable because no SPARQL engine or Python runtime is installed in the environment.
