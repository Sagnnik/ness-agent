Fixed `runtime/shared_heap.c:650`.

The sweep loop was advancing by `Whsize_hd(hd)`, the live object’s actual size, instead of `wh`, the fixed size-class slot width. This caused the scanner to enter padding or fields after run-length-compressed free blocks and corrupt the heap. The loop now advances by `wh`.
