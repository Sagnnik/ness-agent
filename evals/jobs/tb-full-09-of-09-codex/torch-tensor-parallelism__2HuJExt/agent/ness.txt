Implemented `/app/parallel_linear.py` with:

- `ColumnParallelLinear`
  - Shards weights across output features.
  - Shards bias accordingly.
  - Gathers output shards along the last dimension.
- `RowParallelLinear`
  - Shards weights across input features.
  - Expects pre-sharded input.
  - Reduces partial outputs across ranks.
  - Keeps a full bias on every rank.
- Differentiable gather/reduce wrappers to preserve weight and bias gradients.
- Shape and divisibility validation.
- Master weights are cloned and detached before becoming local parameters.
