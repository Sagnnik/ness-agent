Implemented `/app/jump_analyzer.py`.

- Accepts an MP4 path as its command-line argument.
- Locates the fixed hurdle position.
- Uses OpenCV foreground tracking to detect the athlete.
- Detects takeoff and landing from vertical motion relative to the approach baseline.
- Writes `/app/output.toml` with exactly the required fields.

Current example output:

```toml
jump_takeoff_frame_number = 54
jump_land_frame_number = 62
```

Verified with:

```bash
python3 /app/jump_analyzer.py /app/example_video.mp4
```

Also verified that a video with 10 prepended frames shifts both detected frame numbers by 10.
